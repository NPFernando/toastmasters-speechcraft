How to use
More than one roleCovering for someone? Tap ＋ Role to add another role on the same device, then switch with one tap. If the agenda already lists you in several roles, the page offers to add them. While you're in one role, small panels keep your other jobs at hand: the Ah-Counter buttons and the Grammarian's Word of the Day +1 for whoever is speaking, and the Timer's live bar.
Changes on the dayMembers tap In person / Online / Can't make it (with a note like "leaving at 8") so the Coordinator sees clashes and reassigns roles. The Toastmaster can move an item to the end (e.g. a late keynote) or skip it on the run-sheet. 📋 Copy report text (Timer, Ah-Counter, Grammarian, General Evaluator) gives a text summary to paste anywhere, and anyone taking over a role carries on with the same data.
Pick your roleWhen you open the page, choose your role (Timer, Table Topics Master, Ah-Counter or Grammarian), type your name, then host or join the meeting. Your device then shows only your role. Use Change role at the top if you need to switch.
QR codesAfter starting a meeting, a QR code appears: others scan it with their phone camera to open the page with the code filled in. 📱 Open on phone (next to your role) shows a QR code that opens your role on your phone in the same meeting, with all the data.
Connect devicesOne person (usually the Timer) taps Not connected → Host meeting and reads out the 6-character code. Everyone else opens the page, picks their role and types the code (or opens the host's invite link, which fills the code in). Everything then syncs live. Needs internet; the link between devices is encrypted. If someone's connection drops, they keep working and catch up automatically.
Speechcraft CoordinatorProtected by a PIN (4–8 digits). Set once; a one-time recovery code is shown for when the PIN is forgotten. Hand the PIN to the next coordinator, or unlock and use Change PIN. Add members (remembered for every meeting), enter the meeting details, pick role players and edit the agenda. Names fill in on every device and the Timer's lists. 📄 Print agenda makes the agenda sheet.
Toastmaster of the DayShows the agenda with Now and Next. Tap Start the meeting, then Next ▶ as you go (or tap any item). It shows whether the meeting is on time or running behind, and everyone connected sees a small "Now:" line.
General EvaluatorSees the Timer, Ah-Counter and Grammarian results live (Call for reports), which speakers have been evaluated, and writes notes on the meeting. Opens and closes voting.
SpeakerFor prepared speakers (Table Topics speakers are picked on the day). Pick your name and add your speech title, objectives and time limits; you also see your assigned evaluator. The Timer, Toastmaster, evaluators and report show your title, and the Timer uses your limits. See your result, set your feedback PIN and open your private feedback card.
Speech / Table Topic Evaluator⭐ Your speakers (assigned by the Coordinator, or tap Assign to me) appear first, with their speech titles. Type your name, tap a speaker and write Commend · Recommend · Commend plus three quick ratings. It stays a private draft on your device until you tap ✅ Submit evaluation; then it's encrypted so only that speaker can read it. Changed something after? Tap Update submission.
Private feedbackEach speaker sets a feedback PIN once (Member / Audience → My feedback card). Evaluations and audience notes are encrypted for that speaker before they leave the writer's phone; other devices only hold scrambled data, and the General Evaluator and report show only who evaluated whom. Forgot the PIN? The Coordinator can reset it, but older feedback can't be recovered.
VotingWhen the General Evaluator opens voting, a 🗳 Vote bar appears on every device. Pick Best Prepared Speaker, Table Topics Speaker, Evaluator and Role Player (one vote per device per award). Results stay hidden until the Toastmaster taps 🏆 Reveal winners, then they pop up on every screen.
Round Robin MasterAdd questions, tap First question / Next question, show it on screen, and tap each person as they answer: they're queued on the Timer's Round Robin list (20-second bell).
Member / AudienceCheck in when you arrive, vote, send an anonymous note to a speaker, and open My feedback card: your times, Ah count, grammar notes, evaluation, notes and awards on one printable page. The Coordinator sees attendance across all weeks.
Timer: plan speakersType names and press + Add to list (several at once with commas). Press ▶ Start next: Name when they stand up. Rename, reorder ↑ ↓ or remove ✕ any time.
Timer: timingType a name (optional) and press Start speaker. Add or fix the name in the live bar while they speak. Press Stop when done. The live bar flashes SHOW GREEN / AMBER / RED at each signal. If someone is still running when you start another, the page asks first.
Timer: fix or add a time✎ Add time manually for anyone you forgot. Click a finished time to correct it (m:ss). Finished speakers can't be restarted; ✕ removes them (with Undo).
Status meaningUnder time: stopped before green. Qualified: green to red inclusive. Overtime: past red. Round Robin: Within time up to the bell.
Table Topics MasterPaste topics (one per line) and press Add topics. During the session tap 🎲 Random topic or Pick on any topic, then 📺 Show on screen to display it large. Type who takes it and press ▶ Call speaker: the topic moves to Used with their name, and when connected they appear in the Timer's Table Topics list as Next up.
Ah-CounterAdd speakers (or tap + Add next to whoever is speaking now). Select a speaker and tap a word each time you hear it; − undoes one. Edit the word list under Words to count.
GrammarianEnter the Word of the Day. Select a speaker, tap +1 Word of the Day each time they use it, and note great phrases and slips.
ReportMeeting report builds one report. Tick which roles to include, then Print / Save as PDF.
Backup & new meetingData is saved on this device. ⤓ Save backup downloads a file you can ⤒ Restore anywhere. New meeting clears speakers, topics and counts (for connected devices too) but keeps names, time limits and word lists.
